How ServiceHub Works

ServiceHub is a marketplace that connects customers with people and businesses offering digital services.

🔎

Find a Service

Browse available services or use the search bar to find what you need. Explore different service categories and discover providers that match your project.

👀

Explore the Provider

Open a service to learn more about what the provider offers. You can view their service details, starting price, portfolio, rating, and other information they provide.

💬

Contact the Provider

Found a service you like? Use Contact Provider to reach out and discuss your project, requirements, pricing, and other details directly with the provider.

🧑‍💻

Offer Your Services

Are you a freelancer, creator, developer, designer, or business offering a service? Use List Your Service to create your own listing and showcase what you do.

💳

Complete Your Payment

When you're ready to proceed, follow the payment process provided for the service. ServiceHub supports payment options including Naira, USD, and cryptocurrency, depending on the available payment flow.

🛠️

Manage Your Service

Once your listing payment is confirmed, you get a private manage link. It is shown on screen and emailed to you if you gave an email address. Keep it safe, because anyone with this link can read your chats and edit your listing.

Tap Manage Your Service in the menu and paste your link to open your dashboard. There you can update your portfolio, media and contact details, read and reply to chats from customers, and change your price. Your current subscription lasts 6 months.

Lost your link? Contact support on WhatsApp and we will help you get it back.

📋

List a Job

Not finding the service you need?, list a job, and let service providers find you.

🚩

Report, Feedback & Support

Spotted something wrong, or loved a service? Open a listing and tap Report to flag it as spam or fake / misleading, confirm it was genuine, say you loved the service, or leave your own note. Your feedback is linked to that listing so our team can review it.

For anything else, use the Support & Feedback button in the menu. Send our team a suggestion or a complaint, or contact support privately through a supporter, or publicly through our Telegram channel.

⚠️

Listings Are Not Necessarily Verified

A listing being on ServiceHub does not mean it is genuine or verified. Read the details carefully, ask questions, and check out the other party before you agree to anything.

Never pay, or share passwords or one-time codes, until you are sure. If something looks fake or suspicious, use Report to let us know.

In Short

Discover → Explore → Contact → Work Together

ServiceHub is built to make finding digital services easier while giving service providers a place to showcase their skills and connect with potential customers.
